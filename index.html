Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Everything you need to begin exploring your future home with confidence.

Where is Amrit Aarogyam Phase-II located?

The site office is at Roorkee–Haridwar NH-58, opposite Crystal World, near Patanjali Yogpeeth, Bhadrabad, Haridwar, Uttarakhand.

What types of residences are offered?

The developer's website describes 1, 2 and 3 BHK apartments and penthouses. Confirm present availability with the sales team.

Is Amrit Aarogyam RERA registered?

The official project website lists RERA registration UKREP08230000516. Buyers should independently verify the current registration details on the Uttarakhand RERA portal.

Can I get the latest price list and floor plans?

Yes. Use the enquiry form or call the project contact number to request current pricing, floor plans and available inventory. Historic price lists should not be treated as current quotations.

Can I arrange a site visit?

Use the Book a Site Visit button to request a callback, or contact the site office directly to coordinate timing.

Has Phase-I been delivered?

The developer's official website states that Phase-I was successfully delivered. Ask the project team for current construction and possession details for Phase-II.
